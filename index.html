Yilong Wang

M.Sc. student in Computer Science · TU Berlin

I am a master's student in computer science at TU Berlin, supervised by Qianli Wang. I wrote my bachelor's thesis with Qianli Wang and Nils Feldhus.

My research currently focuses on multilingual language models, LLM agents, post-training, and looped transformers. My earlier work studied interpretability and counterfactual explanations for multilingual models.

Looking for internship and PhD positions starting in 2027. Feel free to reach out by email.
